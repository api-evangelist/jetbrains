Today, we’re introducing JetBrains Air Teams – the team layer for agentic development. It gives humans and agents the shared context, environments, tools, and instructions they need to work effectively together across the full development lifecycle. Air Teams is already available to JetBrains business customers, with plans to expand access to individual customers later.
